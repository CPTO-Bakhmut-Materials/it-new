# Урок 1. Комп'ютерна графіка (Krita)

**Time:** 35 min — theory 10, step-by-step walkthrough 25 (test is separate, not counted)
**Goal:** the student can draw a simple picture with layers and text, fix a photo (crop, size, brightness) and save it in the right format.
**Sample files:** `files/photo.jpg` (a free-licence landscape photo, 3000×2000, slightly dark, so cropping, resizing and brightening all make sense)

Menu labels below are in Ukrainian. The exact wording is checked against the real Krita 6 interface during recording.

## Theory

### On the lesson page (2 pictures)

- Pixel and raster image (zoom in on a photo → squares)
- Raster vs vector: a picture comparing the two
- Formats:
  - JPG: photos, smaller files
  - PNG: transparency, sharp edges
  - KRA: Krita project that keeps the layers
- "Save" (project) vs "Export" (finished picture)

### «Детальніше» page (`more.md`, optional, not in the test) ✅

Written: 8 sections (pixel and colour codes, RGB/CMYK, DPI, compression, formats, SVG, tablet, licences), pictures and charts from `recording/more_l1.py`. Original topic list:

- Resolution and DPI: screen vs print (72 vs 300 dpi)
- Colour models: RGB (screen) vs CMYK (print)
- Why saving a JPG again and again lowers its quality (lossy compression)
- SVG and vector programs (Inkscape)
- Other formats: GIF, WebP
- Graphics tablet: why artists use one

## Додаткове завдання (необов'язкове)

_Not required and not done in class. If a student submits it, the teacher grades it. Marked in the book as «Необов'язково»; the book also explains how to submit it._

Make a **postcard (листівка)**:
- background fill;
- a simple drawing on a separate layer;
- a greeting text;
- export it as PNG.

Optional: add the edited photo to the postcard as a new layer.

## Summary (in the lesson)

Raster is made of pixels; layers let you change one part without touching the rest; JPG is for photos, PNG for transparency and sharp edges, KRA is the working project; export the finished picture, save the project.

## Test topics (10 questions)

1. What a pixel is

5. Why to save as .kra
6. What layers are for
7. What Ctrl+Z does
8. What cropping is
9. Which tool fills an area with colour
